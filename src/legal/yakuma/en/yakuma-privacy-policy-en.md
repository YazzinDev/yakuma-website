## 4. Spam Protection with hCaptcha

The contact form is protected against automated submissions, spam and abuse using **hCaptcha**, a service provided by Intuition Machines, Inc., United States.

For this purpose, hCaptcha processes information necessary to determine whether an interaction originates from a person or an automated system. This may include technical and interaction information such as your IP address, browser and device information, timestamps and interaction data.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is protecting the contact form and our infrastructure against spam, automated abuse and malicious activity.

Where information is stored on or accessed from your device solely to provide this security functionality, such processing is limited to technologies necessary to provide and secure the requested functionality.

Intuition Machines uses recognised safeguards for applicable international data transfers, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

The hCaptcha widget loads when you approach the contact form or try to submit an inquiry. This may establish a connection to hCaptcha even if you do not send an inquiry.

Further information is available in the [hCaptcha Privacy Policy](https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy).
